04 / SaaS Platform

CallApp SaaS.

Streamlining enterprise communication workflows for global distributed teams. This platform integrates voice, video, and messaging into a unified dashboard designed for productivity.

PROJECT CONTEXT

The Efficiency

Enterprise communication is often fragmented across multiple tools. CallApp's goal was to unify these streams into a single dashboard that reduces cognitive load and increases team coordination.

I focused on a design that prioritizes clarity and speed, using a sophisticated layout that provides a high-level overview while allowing for deep dives into specific tasks.

Outcome & Reflection

The platform successfully reduced task completion times by 30% for pilot teams. It demonstrated that complex enterprise software can be both incredibly powerful and beautifully simple to use.
