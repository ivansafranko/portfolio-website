Case Study / 2024
02 / Mobile Application

Moj OPG Marketplace.

Connecting local organic farmers directly with urban consumers. A comprehensive digital ecosystem built to simplify the supply chain and ensure fair trade for local producers.

PROJECT CONTEXT

The Challenge

Farmers in Croatia often struggle with reaching urban customers without intermediaries taking significant margins. Conversely, consumers want fresh, organic food but find it difficult to verify sources and arrange convenient deliveries.

The goal was to create a seamless mobile-first experience that builds trust through transparency and makes ordering farm-fresh produce as easy as ordering a pizza.

Outcome & Reflection

Moj OPG successfully launched in the Croatian market, becoming the leading platform for farm-to-table commerce. The project demonstrated the power of a user-centric design approach in solving complex logistics and trust issues in a traditional industry.
